          Fleets

          Switched-on fleets are shown on the map with their latest trip. Switching one off stops following it. Rescue boats, icebreakers, cruise ships and harbour boats start switched off.

          Notifications

          Chart storage

          Draws the drawn chart outside Finnish waters too, from EMODnet depths and OpenStreetMap sea marks (less detail than the Finnish side). Turn it off to save about 5 to 10 MB of memory.

          Adds rocks, wrecks and place names to the drawn chart. Get a free key from Maanmittauslaitos.

          Troubleshooting

          Long press the drawn chart where it looks wrong: what the chart has there (tiles at zoom 8 to 12 and the depth areas) is saved to Home Assistant's Media folder, under ferry_watch › chart-samples, with a download link. Send the file in so it can be fixed.
